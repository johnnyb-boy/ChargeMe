# ChargeMe
## An simple app to force charge on your calculator.

### What do ChargeMe?
**ChargeMe** show nothing (exept "The calculator is ON.") to let her charge without going in sleep mode.

### Why using ChargeMe?
This app is made for some **Numworks calculator** who have a error that cut the charge when she's in sleep mode

This Extra-App was made and compiled in C and by only using the ```<eadk.h>``` official librairy by Numworks / Epsilon.

### How to install it?
Download ```ChargeMe_EN.nwa``` on your calculator using the **[Official Numworks Extra-Apps installer](https://my.numworks.com/apps)** (a **Chromium (Chrome, Brave, Opera, ect...)** is required for this action), after this, launch it from the calculator and *There you go*
### **Controls**
**Exit**: Hold Home button
